Terms, Policies & Parental Consent

Children of War Project (“CoW,” “we,” “us”) · Last updated September 21, 2026

Children of War Project is a student-led initiative founded by Yuvraaj Kumar, Dhanvin Challa, and Guntas Dhanjal to raise funds and awareness for children affected by war, homelessness, loneliness, and poverty. This page explains how we operate, how funds are used, and what participation involves — especially for participants under 18.

1. Our nonprofit status

CoW is organized as a New Jersey nonprofit and has applied for 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status. Status: pending

Until our 501(c)(3) determination is approved, donations to CoW are not guaranteed to be tax-deductible. Please consult your own tax advisor. We will update this page the moment our status changes.

2. How funds are used

100% of net proceeds we raise — from coin collection, garage sales, book resales, and donations — are directed to trusted, established charities serving children affected by war and poverty, including Voices of Children (Ukraine), selected and reviewed by our Board of Trustees & Advisors. CoW does not retain any portion of donations for operating expenses; any incidental costs (materials, printing, event supplies) are covered separately by our student team or in-kind sponsorship.

3. Our annual report promise

Each year, CoW will publish a simple annual report showing total funds raised, where they were sent, and the impact of our partner organizations' work, so every supporter can see exactly how their contribution was used.

4. Parental & guardian consent

CoW welcomes participants of all ages as Volunteers, Supporters, Activists, Ambassadors, and Change Makers. If you are a minor (under the age of 18, or under the age of majority in your state or country), you must have the permission of a parent or legal guardian before volunteering, fundraising, donating, or submitting any personal information through our sign-up form.

  • Our sign-up form requires an affirmative consent confirmation before it can be submitted by or on behalf of a minor.
  • Parents/guardians may contact us at any time to review, correct, or remove information submitted about their child.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verified parental consent.

5. Limitation of responsibility

CoW is a volunteer, student-led effort. While we take care in selecting reputable partner organizations, we cannot control how those third-party organizations ultimately use donated funds once transferred, and we make no guarantees beyond the reporting described above. Participation in CoW events (garage sales, community tables, fundraising activities) is voluntary, and participants and their parents/guardians assume responsibility for their own safety and conduct at such events. CoW, its student founders, and volunteers are not liable for any loss, injury, or damages arising from participation, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

6. Information we collect & privacy

Information submitted through our sign-up form (name, email, phone, location, and how you'd like to participate) is used only to coordinate volunteering, fundraising, and Change Maker recognition, and is not sold or shared with third parties outside CoW's own coordination (e.g., our Google Form/Sheet). You may request removal of your information at any time by emailing us below.

7. Contact us

Questions about these terms, our fund use, or your data? Email us at hello.childrenofwarproject@gmail.com.
